Privacy Policy for Quran Orbit

Last Updated: November 2026

Quran Orbit is an Islamic utility application provided as a free service. This page informs users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information.

1. Information Collection and Storage

Quran Orbit does not collect, track, or share any personally identifiable information (PII). Features such as prayer times, Qibla compass, Tasbih counter, Zakat calculator, and offline Quran audio downloads operate locally on your device. User preferences and downloaded audio files are stored internally on your device and are not transmitted to external servers.

2. Permissions & Connectivity

The application requires network access solely to load Quranic and related Islamic content and download audio for offline playback. Location access (if granted) is used strictly on-device to calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction. Foreground services are used only for uninterrupted Quran audio playback and downloading surahs for offline access. The app does not access your sensitive private files, contacts, or camera.

3. Third-Party Services

The app may utilize standard Google Play Services for operational stability. These services do not collect personal identifying information from this app.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Users are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
